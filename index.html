🎂 Birthday Reminder

Contact search works only in Google Chrome on Android. If you opened this link inside WhatsApp, tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome. You can still type names and numbers yourself.

Get a WhatsApp message every morning

At 6:30 AM, if a friend or relative has a birthday or marriage anniversary that day, you get a WhatsApp message. No login needed.

Step 1: Get your free WhatsApp key

  1. Open CallMeBot's page and save the phone number shown there in your contacts.
  2. On WhatsApp, send that contact this exact message:
    I allow callmebot to send me messages
  3. You will get a reply with your API key (a number like 123456). Copy it.

Step 2: Fill this in

Already using this on another phone? Enter the same number and key and your list comes back.

Add a birthday or anniversary

Get dates your friends already saved

Choose people from your phone contacts. If any friend using this app has saved their birthday or anniversary, you can add it to your list in one tap. Only numbers that are in your contacts are matched.

Tip: in the contact list, use the search or the "select all" option if your phone shows one.
